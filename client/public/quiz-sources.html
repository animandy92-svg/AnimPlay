Good questions deserve credit.

The AnimPlay collection contains 33 quizzes and 535 questions: 450 imported trivia questions, 20 flag-identification bonus questions, 20 original chart-reading questions, and 45 original questions in Flags, Countries & Capitals, and Animals quizzes.

Trivia: Open Trivia Database

450 multiple-choice questions and their answer keys were retrieved from the Open Trivia Database public API on September 29, 2026. The database is contributed by the Open Trivia DB community and operated by PIXELTAIL GAMES LLC.

Question data is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution–ShareAlike 4.0 International. AnimPlay decoded text, grouped questions into themed quizzes, reordered answers, and added visual rounds. No endorsement by the source is implied.

Flag pictures

Flag PNGs were downloaded from FlagCDN / Flagpedia, whose images are based on Wikimedia Commons vector files. The depicted national flag designs are public domain. The collection includes Japan, France, Brazil, Canada, Italy, Switzerland, India, the United Kingdom, South Africa, and Australia. AnimPlay wrote the associated identification questions. These images are stored with the app so live rounds do not depend on third-party image requests.

Focused categories

The Flags, Countries & Capitals, and Animals quizzes each contain 15 questions written by AnimPlay. The Flags quiz uses local flag images from FlagCDN / Flagpedia. Original questions are released under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Chart diagrams

The ten chart diagrams and their 20 questions are original AnimPlay additions, released under CC BY-SA 4.0. The charts use fictional survey data for practicing comparison and subtraction; they do not claim to represent real surveys.
