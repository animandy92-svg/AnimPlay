Simple questions. Clear topics.

The beginner collection contains eight quizzes and 120 questions: Cars & Symbols, Bible Characters, Countries & Capitals, Countries & Flags, Accounting Standards, Simple Riddles, Animal Pictures, and Everyday General Knowledge. Each quiz has 15 questions entirely within its named topic.

Original questions

Questions are written by AnimPlay in plain language, with four choices and one correct answer. They are released under CC BY-SA 4.0. The builder selects questions only from the chosen category. Hosts can preview and edit their copies.

Download the collection and per-question references (JSON)

Bible characters

Questions cover well-known people from Bible stories. Each question links to its relevant passage in the King James Version through Bible Gateway. Questions use original wording.

Accounting standards

Beginner questions introduce standards and their subject areas, using IFRS Foundation references and FASB for GAAP. They use original wording and simple examples, with no technical calculations.

Flag pictures

The 15 national flag PNGs come from FlagCDN / Flagpedia, based on Wikimedia Commons vector files. The depicted national flag designs are public domain. They are stored locally so games do not depend on external image requests.

Animal pictures

All 15 animal pictures are real photographs from Wikimedia Commons. Each photo is stored locally, with its photographer and individual license credited in the question. The source links include full licensing details. Earlier drawings in saved copies display the matching photograph.

Earlier collection

Existing hosts may retain earlier quizzes they edited, favourited, filed or moved to trash. That collection included 450 questions retrieved from Open Trivia Database on September 29, 2026, contributed by its community and operated by PIXELTAIL GAMES LLC, under CC BY-SA 4.0. AnimPlay decoded the text, reordered answers and grouped rounds. The earlier original chart questions use fictional survey data. Untouched earlier defaults are hidden from the library but retained for existing links and assignments.
